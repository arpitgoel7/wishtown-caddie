How to read your caddie

  1. Choose a hole with the ‹ › arrows or the Hole list at the top. Your tee (Blue for men, Red for ladies), the pin (front, middle, back) and the goal are next to it. Play to handicap safely avoids big numbers; Lowest average chases the best score.
  2. Game plan is your caddie for that hole: the four boxes show your average score, how often you make net par or better, your average Stableford points and the blow-up risk (net double bogey or worse). Below that: the tee club and where to aim (and where to start the ball if you draw or fade it), then the next shot, your likely scores, and the other tee clubs it tried. Tap a row to see that club on the map.
  3. The map: the yellow line is your route, each dot is where a shot finishes.
    Your routeFairwayRoughTreesBunkerWater / OBGreen
    White arcs are distances from your tee.
  4. Round plan lists your tee club, aim, average and Stableford on all 18 holes, and the strokes you get on each. Tap a hole to open it.
  5. My bag is where you add or edit your clubs: carry and total distance, side miss (± yds), confidence, and your shot shape (draw, straight or fade, and any club that curves differently). Until you enter yours, the plan uses sample numbers for your handicap. Your bag is saved on this phone only.
  6. Hazards lists every bunker, pond, tree line and OB with the distance to reach and to carry it from your tee. Scorecard has the card, ratings and your strokes per hole.

The plan is an estimate from satellite maps and a golf model, not a guarantee. Yardages follow the card except where a hole says otherwise (hole 1's tees are currently set forward and are approximate). Always check the markers on the day.
